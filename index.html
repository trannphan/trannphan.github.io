I'm an Assistant Professor at the Department of Research on the Languages and Cultures of Taiwan and Vietnam, Faculty of Ethnolinguistics, Adam Mickiewicz University in Poznań, Poland. My name is pronounced /ʈə̆n˨˩ fan˧/. I also go by Phan Ngọc Trần in my home country, and 潘玉陳 in the Chinese-speaking world.

My current research interests include non-canonical wh-interrogatives, mood and modality, negation, scalarity and additivity, finiteness distinctions, similative and associative plurality, grammaticalization, and Old Vietnamese, among others.
